Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains clearly how we handle and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on Bristol Community Events, we collect:

• Full name • Email address • Password (encrypted and never stored in plain text)

We do not collect personal information unless you choose to provide it.

2. How Your Information Is Used

We use your information to:

• Create and manage your user account • Allow you to book or view community events • Send updates only if you opt in

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data Protection & Security

We take data protection seriously. Your personal information is stored securely using encrypted and safe storage methods. While no system is 100% secure, we follow best practices to reduce risks.

4. Cookies

This website uses only essential cookies required for functionality. We do not use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

5. Your Rights

Under privacy regulations, you have the right to:

• Request access to your stored information • Correct or update your personal data • Request account deletion

To use any of these rights, contact us through our Contact page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:

Email: support@bristolevents.co.uk
